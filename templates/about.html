Why 311Assist?

A tool to make reviewing 311 cases in Houston clearer and more efficient for 311 agents.

What it does

311Assist helps Houston 311 staff look up existing records by case number or complete street address. It brings the recorded status, department, dates, and notes together in one place.

Important limits

The results come from a data snapshot, not a live connection to Houston 311. The app cannot submit or change service requests. Please verify current information before taking action.
